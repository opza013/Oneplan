# ONE PLAN v5.4.20 — Key Milestone Master Reorder

- Added quick **Move Up (↑)** and **Move Down (↓)** controls to **Key Milestone Master**.
- Reordering immediately re-indexes `Display Order` sequentially and saves the updated master state.
- First milestone disables Move Up; last milestone disables Move Down.
- Existing Edit `Display Order`, multi-PIC/NWT assignments, dependencies, colors, and usage protections remain unchanged.
- Reorder controls work in both normal and expanded Master Data views.
